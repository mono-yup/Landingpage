Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

See Your Yard is a free yard preview service of Flores Landscape & Services LLC ("we," "us").This page explains what we collect when you ask for a preview, what we do with it, and what you can ask us to do.

What we collect

Your first name, your mobile number, and the photo of your yard you upload. We also keep a record of your text-message consent (the date, the time, and the page you submitted it on) and standard visit data such as pages viewed and device type.

How we use it

To make your preview, to text it to you, to follow up about your project, and to give you a quote if you ask for one. We don't sell your information.

Your photo and AI processing

To make the preview, we send your photo to a third-party AI image service (Google) that processes it for us. We keep your photo and your preview for 90 days so we can follow up with you, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time. We won't publish your photo or your preview anywhere without your written permission.

Text messages

If you check the consent box on our form, Flores Landscape & Services LLC will text you at the mobile number you entered: your preview, and follow-up about your project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and text-message consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who else handles your information

We use service providers to run this: our texting and contact-management platform, our AI image provider, and our website hosting. They handle your information only to do that work for us.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP. You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete what we have about you by contacting us below.
